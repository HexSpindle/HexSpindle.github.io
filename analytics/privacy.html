HexSpindle — Analytics Privacy Notice

← Back to HexSpindle

HexSpindle processes recipes and forensic evidence locally in your browser. Site-usage analytics are separate and never receive your forensic inputs or outputs.

Information collected

HexSpindle collects usage analytics only when your browser is not signaling Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track (DNT), and you have not opted out through the website's analytics controls. When analytics are enabled, the information collected includes your connecting public IP address (recorded by Cloudflare), browser visitor and session identifiers, timestamps, operation names and categories, events indicating whether operations were selected or executed, generic success/error status, operation duration, and high-level feature usage. Forensic inputs, outputs, uploaded files, and other analysis content are never included in analytics.

Information not collected

Forensic inputs, outputs, uploaded files, filenames, API keys, recipe contents or arguments, search text, clipboard contents, or data being transformed.

Purpose, storage, and access

The operator uses analytics to understand usage, troubleshoot errors, and prioritize development.

Opt out and deletion

Use Opt out of analytics in the website footer to stop future telemetry from this browser; this also clears its analytics identifiers in local and session storage. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are respected.
